KEY BUSINESS RISK

EMEA Profitability Gap

5.45%

EMEA recorded the lowest profit margin across all markets, significantly below the global average.

Potential areas for investigation:
  • Pricing strategy
  • Discount levels
  • Product mix
  • Operational costs
ANALYST FINDINGS

What the Data Tells Us

Key findings translated from data into business context.

01

Revenue Nearly Doubled

Annual sales increased from $2.26M in 2011 to $4.30M in 2014, demonstrating consistent business expansion.

Growth Opportunity
02

EMEA Requires Attention

EMEA generated the lowest profit margin at 5.45%, less than half the profitability achieved by the strongest markets.

Profitability Risk
03

Q4 Drives Peak Performance

Q4 generated the highest sales, profit, and order volume, highlighting strong year-end seasonality.

Seasonal Pattern
04

Revenue ≠ Maximum Profit

December generated the highest sales, while November generated the highest profit, suggesting differences in discounting or product mix.
