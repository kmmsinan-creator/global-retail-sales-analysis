BUSINESS INSIGHTS

What the data is telling us

Key analytical findings translated into business-focused observations and decision opportunities.

INSIGHT 01

Sustained multi-year growth

Revenue increased from approximately $2.26M in 2011 to $4.30M in 2014, demonstrating strong and sustained business expansion across the analysis period.

Growth Opportunity
INSIGHT 02

Year-end demand is strongest

November and December generated the highest monthly sales, indicating a clear seasonal demand pattern that should influence inventory and campaign planning.

Seasonal Planning
INSIGHT 03

APAC is the largest revenue market

APAC generated approximately $3.59M in sales, making it the strongest revenue contributor across the six global markets analyzed.

Market Leadership
INSIGHT 04

EMEA requires margin investigation

Despite generating over $800K in revenue, EMEA produced a profit margin of only 5.45%, significantly below other markets and requiring deeper investigation.

Margin Risk
